Behind the art

Michael Maria is a contemporary artist based in Dortmund, Germany. His works bring personal experience into dialogue with faith, identity and human vulnerability.

Text, colour and spiritual symbolism shape his visual language. Early works, darker intermediate phases and recent pieces trace a journey through inner conflict towards hope and trust. A recurring boot motif serves as a personal signature.
